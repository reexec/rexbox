# 작업 로그: DSOUND 음성 headroom과 3D 음성 (issue #40)

작업 지시: [20261102-i040-dsound-3d](../work-orders/20261102-i040-dsound-3d.md) · 설계: [20261102-i040-dsound-3d](../design/20261102-i040-dsound-3d.md) · 소비자: reexec/reCT3#33

## 한 일

1. CT3 데모 160초의 DSOUND 호출 수와 인자를 진단 빌드(커밋하지 않음)로 모았다. 3D 음성 약 50개, `SetPosition`·`SetVelocity` 각 약 2,500회, headroom은 효과음 600, 음악 0이었다(설계 §1).
2. CT3에 정적 링크된 XDK 4531 DSOUND를 디스어셈블해 headroom 의미와 기본값, 거리 감쇠 식, head-relative 처리를 확인했다(설계 §2).
3. headroom: 생성 때 기본값(2D 600, 3D·submix 0), `volume − headroom`, `SetHeadroom`이 이득을 다시 계산한다.
4. `dsound_3d.cpp`: 버퍼 3D 설정 10개와 리스너 설정 6개, `CommitDeferredSettings`가 값을 저장하고 다시 계산한다. 거리 감쇠(XDK 식), cone, 균형 법칙 패닝, 도플러 비를 계산한다. 리스너 방향이 퇴화하면 기본 축을 쓴다(probe가 0 벡터로 부르는 경우에서 찾음).
5. 믹서: 3D 음성은 스피커 이득의 평균에 감쇠와 패닝을 곱하고, 도플러 비를 재생 간격에 곱한다.
6. 사용자 청취 1차: "예전과 비슷하고 차량 효과음만 강조된다". 음원별 믹스 빈을 집계해 보니 2D 모노 효과음이 FL·FR·LFE·BL·BR에 모두 0 dB로 보낸다. 진폭 합으로 접으면 음악보다 +8.6 dB(headroom 뒤에도 +2.6 dB)였다. 스테레오 접기를 전력 합(`√Σg²`), 가운데 −3 dB, LFE 제외로 바꿨다. 이 효과음은 음악 대비 −3 dB가 된다.
7. 사용자 청취 2차: 효과음이 아직 어색하지만 이 단계에서 정리하기로 했다.
8. dsound probe: headroom 3개, 스테레오 접기 2개, 3D 13개를 더했다(55 → 73). 기존 검사가 볼륨만 보도록 probe의 버퍼 생성 도우미는 headroom을 0으로 맞춘다.

# Work log: DSOUND voice headroom and 3D voices (issue #40)

Work order: [20261102-i040-dsound-3d](../work-orders/20261102-i040-dsound-3d.md) · Design: [20261102-i040-dsound-3d](../design/20261102-i040-dsound-3d.md) · Consumer: reexec/reCT3#33

Done: DSOUND call counts and arguments over 160 s of CT3's demo were gathered with a diagnostic build (not committed): about 50 3D voices, about 2,500 `SetPosition` and `SetVelocity` calls each, headroom 600 on effects and 0 on the music (design section 1). Disassembling CT3's statically linked XDK 4531 DSOUND confirmed the headroom semantics and defaults, the distance formula and the head-relative handling (design section 2). Headroom: the creation defaults (2D 600, 3D and submix 0), `volume − headroom`, and `SetHeadroom` re-deriving the gains. `dsound_3d.cpp`: ten buffer 3D settings, six listener settings and `CommitDeferredSettings` store and recompute; the XDK distance attenuation, the cone, a balance-law pan and the Doppler ratio; a degenerate listener orientation falls back to the default axes (found through the probe's zero-vector calls). Mixer: a 3D voice takes the mean of its speaker gains times the attenuation and pan, and the Doppler ratio multiplies its step. First listening: "much as before, only the car effects stand out"; counting each voice's mix bins showed the 2D mono effects sending 0 dB to FL, FR, LFE, BL and BR, which the amplitude fold made 8.6 dB louder than the music (2.6 dB after the headroom); the stereo fold became a power sum (`√Σg²`) with the center at −3 dB and the LFE left out, putting those effects 3 dB under the music. Second listening: the effects still sound off, but the work is wrapped up at this stage. The dsound probe gains 3 headroom, 2 fold-down and 13 3D checks (55 → 73); its buffer helper sets headroom 0 so the earlier checks read the volume alone.

Verified: dsound probe 73/73; the consumer reCT3 Linux x64 Release with 0 warnings and CTest 7/7; a hidden 120 s reCT3 demo without a stop at about 59 fps; listening on Linux: the effects stand out less than before but still sound off; Win32 through the pull request's CI.

Left open: the effects still sound off, with the original Xbox stereo downmix (the EP DSP), HRTF, I3DL2 reverb, gain ramps and resampling quality (pitched engine sounds reach 70 kHz) as next candidates; Doppler and the cone follow DirectSound's definitions, the original formulas unchecked; reCT3's music repeat (reexec/reCT3#29) is unrelated to this work.
