이전 릴리스 **v0.0.16** 이후의 변경입니다(issue #38). **Hor+ 와이드스크린에서 넓힌 깊이 스텐실과 함께 묶인 4:3 색 타깃도 넓힙니다.** 텍스처 렌더 타깃이 깊이를 4:3 x 자리에 써서, 이후 장면 그리기에 구멍이 났습니다(소비자 reCT3: 밤 운전 중 헤드라이트 왼쪽 240픽셀에 검은 점 두 개).

## rexbox v0.0.17 (English)

Changes since **v0.0.16** (issue #38). **Under Hor+ widescreen a 4:3 color target bound with the widened depth stencil is widened too.** A texture render target wrote its depth at the 4:3 x, leaving holes in later scene draws (in the consumer reCT3, two black dots 240 pixels left of the headlights while driving at night).

Issue: #38, the depth position of a 4:3 color target bound with the widened depth stencil (`7e01c49`, `8e6aa3d`).

Changes: `src/hle/d3d8/d3d8_gl_targets.cpp` records a color target bound at the widened depth stencil's guest size and widens it like a back buffer, reporting a different size once; the sampling copy of a widened target (back buffers included) takes only the 4:3 middle ([design i038](../design/20261029-i038-wide-depth-pairing.md)). `src/hle/d3d8/d3d8_capture.cpp`: capture draw lines gain `zs <depth surface header>`. Documents: `ARCHITECTURE.md`, design, work order and work log i038.

Consumers: move the submodule. The public contract is unchanged and no regeneration is needed; 4:3 is unchanged. An effect that reads the back buffer as a texture at widescreen now reads the 4:3 middle rather than the whole wide picture; check such effects at widescreen.

Verified: in the consumer reCT3, Win32 x86 Release with 0 warnings and `ctest` 7/7; at 21:9 the night-driving dots are gone (user confirmed) and the attract demo ran 150 s.
